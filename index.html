Why Saathiyaa

Saathiyaa comes from saathi, the Hindi and Gujarati word for companion. We built it for the nights you want company, not a crowd.

Made for garba season

Dandiya partners, wedding plus-ones and dinner company, found by activity instead of guesswork.

Verify for a pink tick

ID verification is optional. People who pass an ID and selfie check and a human review get a pink tick next to ID verified on their card.

Both sides choose

Companions accept or decline every meet request and every chat request. No one is ever assigned. You only see, and are only seen by, people who want to meet someone like you.

Safe by design

Every meet request names a public place, chat opens only after the other person accepts, you can share meet details with a friend in one tap, and you can report or block anyone.

Free to use

Chat and meet requests cost nothing while we launch. No money changes hands through Saathiyaa.
