Why Saathi

Saathi means companion. We built it for the nights you want company, not a crowd.

Made for garba season

Dandiya partners, wedding plus-ones and dinner company, booked by activity instead of guesswork.

Verified, every time

Every companion passes an ID and selfie check, then a human review, before they appear as verified.

Both sides choose

Companions accept or decline each request and set their own day or hourly rate. No one is ever assigned.

Safe by design

Every request names a public place, you can share booking details with a friend in one tap, and you can report or block anyone.

Clear, fair pricing

Book by the day or by the hour. The platform fee is shown before you send a request.
